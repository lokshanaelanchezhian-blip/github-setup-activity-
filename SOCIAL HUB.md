## Social networking, explained clearly.

How online communities connect people, share ideas and shape the way we live, learn and work.

#### The basics

### What is it?

Online platforms where people build profiles, connect with others and share posts, photos and videos.

### How it works

You follow or friend people, and algorithms choose which posts appear in your feed.

### Why it matters

It helps with staying in touch, finding jobs, learning skills and growing small businesses.

#### Types of platforms

### Social

Friends and family sharing, like Facebook and Instagram.

### Professional

Careers and networking, like LinkedIn.

### Video

Short and long videos, like YouTube and TikTok.

### Messaging

Private chats and groups, like WhatsApp and Telegram.

#### Stay safe online

### Strong passwords

Use a unique password for each account and turn on two-step login.

### Think before sharing

Avoid posting your address, phone number or other private details.

### Spot fake news

Check the source before you believe or forward something.
